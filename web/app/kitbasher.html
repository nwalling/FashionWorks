FashionWorks

Browse every piece of FPS armour in Star Citizen, mix sets together, and try colourways, all from the game you already have installed. Nothing is uploaded: your browser reads your own copy of the game, on your own computer.

You need Star Citizen installed on the desktop computer you are reading this on, and a few GB of free disk space for a cache that makes return visits instant. Chrome or Edge is recommended, and Firefox is supported. No installs, no command line, and no account.

Hangarworks is an unofficial fan tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cloud Imperium Games.
